— zsh
Maze is a fixed 31 × 28 tile grid. Walls draw as line art resolved from neighbours. It never scales or stretches.

Layout Modes

Standard
< 40 cols
1 cell per tile. The maze reads tall and narrow; the HUD keeps the same rows above and below. The minimal footprint that still shows a complete, unclipped board.
Wide
40 – 79 cols
2 cells per tile — near-arcade proportions. The preferred default. The maze reads much closer to its original 28 × 31 aspect ratio without any stretching of the tiles themselves.
Wide + Rail
80+ cols
Adds a 20-column rail to the right: a live ghost roster, a dots-remaining meter, the current scatter/chase mode, and key hints. The maze itself is unchanged.

The maze never scales or stretches. Only three things change with size: cells-per-tile, whether the rail exists, and the centering offsets. Anything smaller than the minimum shows a live notice instead of a clipped board.
